Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

WAYNE EDGE (“the app”, “we”) is a private trading journal available at wayneedge.wayneexchange.workers.dev. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What we collect

Your email address and password (used to sign in), your name and country, and the journal entries you type in, such as trades, accounts, notes and settings.

Where it is stored

Sign-in details and text data are stored with our database and authentication provider, Supabase, and are protected so that each person can only access their own records. Some data is also kept in your own browser on your device.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks only for the drive.file permission. This lets the app create and open only the files it creates itself, such as your encrypted backups, screenshots and voice notes. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. Files saved to Drive stay in your own Drive and are not copied to our servers. You can disconnect at any time from the app’s Settings, or remove access in your Google Account permissions.

PDF statements and verification

When you create a PDF statement, a verification number is issued so the statement can be checked later. This record contains the statement’s summary details (such as your name, date range and result figures) and a digital signature.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or show ads.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your data inside the app, disconnect Google Drive, or ask us to delete your account and records by contacting us at the email below.
